## Evidence and presentation rules

- The current map and status authority are `198x/docs/project-map.md`, `198x/docs/status.md` and `198x/docs/status-authority.md`. The older July Codex map predates Isa198x/Debug198x graduation and Play198x implementation; do not repeat its stale lifecycle labels.
- Studio198x is absent from the quick project map but explicitly established in `198x/decisions/studio198x-authoring.md`. It is not started. The decision, not the absence of a local directory, governs its inclusion as a planned sibling.
- Proposed presentation: 16 full editions, including the four unpublished prototypes; 198x is a labelled group of nine active peers. Forge198x and Studio198x may appear as two quiet planned notebook entries, approved by Steve. New entries add rows; no fixed item count or pagination is required.
- The 198x siblings are independent peers, not subordinate features of Code198x. All remain free and non-commercial. Source: `sibling-project-coordination.md`, `sibling-cross-promotion.md`, `the-project-is-free-and-non-commercial.md` in the umbrella decisions folder.
- Preserve the real 198x identity artwork when producing final covers. Phosphene's cover treatment does not authorise replacing sibling logos; `198x/decisions/family-visual-identity.md` specifies real Oxanium glyphs and the Code Like It's 198x naming. Existing mockups show provisional lettering.
- Before implementation, verify each destination against current public websites or GitHub organisations. Prefer an existing project website; otherwise the organisation, as required by the cross-promotion decision. No links to unstarted products or invented downloads.
- This review used local documentation. No sibling was rebuilt or retested, and readiness labels do not certify every claimed capability.
