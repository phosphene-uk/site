## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors curious about the games, tools and experiments made under Phosphene. More specific audience segments remain open.

## Product Purpose

A studio showcase that makes people curious about the work and leads them into the individual projects. Steve confirmed this purpose on 2026-10-13.

## Positioning

Phosphene is where Steve Hill builds games, emulation tools, teaching material for classic machines and an experimental operating system. The existing site's description is “Made carefully, and mostly for the love of it.”

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing implementation: one static page at `public/index.html`, without a build step or package dependencies.
- `README.md` records Cloudflare Pages deployment on push. Preserve this deployment model unless a change is agreed.
- Preserve working project links, email contact and legal attribution.
- Verify current release and platform claims before rewriting or expanding them; existing copy is evidence of prior wording, not proof of current status.
- No invented testimonials, customers, usage figures or product screenshots.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the Phosphene name.
- Steve explicitly opened all visual elements to change, including amber, the glow, lowercase typography and the studio lockup.
- The request is to evolve a previously good but dull brand and website.
- Steve chose Software editions with restrained Studio notebook details as the new identity direction.
- The catalogue must accommodate additional projects; Steve rejected the apparent fixed capacity of the five-project compositions. Do not treat five editions as a permanent layout limit.

## Product Principles

- Make the work itself the reason to explore.
- Express the range of projects while giving the studio a recognisable identity.
- Keep navigation to each project direct and accessible.
- Make claims supported by the actual projects.
